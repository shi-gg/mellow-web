The Styles feature enables you to customize (or whitelabel) Wamellow's appearance by modifying its username, avatar, banner, and bio. These customizations apply across all bot interactions, including commands, features, and the server member list.

![wamellow whitelabeled command](/docs-assets/bot-style.webp)

## Setup

1. Add Wamellow to your server by going to [wamellow.com/add](https://wamellow.com/add).
2. Head to the dashboard by going to [wamellow.com/dashboard](https://wamellow.com/dashboard).
3. Select your server from the dashboard.
4. Click **Change Style** at the top of the dashboard.
5. Customize the username, avatar, banner, bio, display name font and effect.
6. **🎉 Done!** The new style will be visible across all bot interactions and in the server member list.

## Username, Avatar, Banner & Bio

- **Username**: 2–32 characters, must not contain `@`, `#`, `:`, ```` ``` ````, `discord` or `clyde` and cannot be `everyone` or `here`.
- **Avatar**: `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, `.webp` or `.apng`, max 8MiB.
- **Banner**: `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, `.webp` or `.apng`, max 8MiB.
- **Bio**: Up to 190 characters.

You can also **Delete** the style to revert to the default Wamellow appearance.
