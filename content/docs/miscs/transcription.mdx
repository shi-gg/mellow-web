---
title: 🎙️ Transcribe Voice Messages
description: Transcribe voice messages and audio-files in Discord.
---

You can transcribe voice messages and audio-files by right-clicking on the message -> "Apps" -> "Wamellow" and selecting "Transcribe Voice".
