---
title: 🎙️ Transcribe Voice Messages
description: Transcribe voice messages and audio-files in Discord.
---

You can transcribe voice messages and audio-files by right-clicking on the message -> "Apps" -> "Wamellow" and selecting "Transcribe Voice".

![Transcribed Voice Message: "You've got mail"](/docs-assets/transcribe-voice-message.webp)

## Automatic Transcription

Automatic transcription is available for voice messages and can be enabled in [the dashboard](/profile?to=/) (scroll to the bottom on the "Overview" tab) for everyone in the server.
