---
title: 🎞️ Convert To Gif
description: Convert images to gifs directly inside Discord.
---

You can convert images to gifs by right-clicking on the image -> "Apps" -> "Wamellow" and selecting "Convert To Gif".

This allows you to favorite the gif (the original image) and save it to your library for quick shit-posting access.
- **Desktop**: Hover over the image and click the `⭐` to favorite the gif
- **Mobile**: Tap the image and tab the `⭐` in the top right corner to favorite the gif
