## 🕵️‍♀️ Styles (whitelabel)

The Styles feature enables you to customize (or whitelabel) Wamellow's appearance by modifying its username, avatar, banner, and bio. These customizations apply across all bot interactions, including commands, features, and the server member list.

The username must follow the [name constraints set by Discord](https://discord.com/developers/docs/resources/user#usernames-and-nicknames). The avatar must be a `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, or `.webp` file and be less than 8MiB in size. (Animated formats like GIFs are also supported)

## Professionalism & Disable Commands

Not every command is a fit for every server. If you find commands like `/anime`, `/blahaj`, `/ask` and `/meow` unnecessary, you can disable them via Discord's own Integration settings. To do this, click your server's name, go to "Server Settings," then select "Integrations" under the Apps category. Find Wamellow in the list, click "Manage", and you can restrict commands by member, role or channel. You can watch a visual guide at [wamellow.com/disable-commands](/disable-commands).

Wamellow does not offer generative AI commands (like `/image`) in order to maintain a safe and ethical environment.

## Security

Wamellow develops and maintains various tools, utilities, and APIs in-house on its own infrastructure. While we prioritize security, we recognize that no system is entirely without risk. We welcome the responsible discovery and reporting of vulnerabilities in our programs and infrastructure, provided that you do not seek personal gain, cause harm, abuse the system, disrupt services, or disclose the issue to third parties before it is resolved.

Please report any findings to [luna@wamellow.com](mailto:luna@wamellow.com) or **mwlica** on Discord. We do not accept reports regarding: brute force or DDoS attacks; publicly accessible login panels; automated scanner reports; the subdomain status.wamellow.com; self-exploitation; or social engineering of users.

For maximum server security, we strongly advise against granting Wamellow "Administrator" permissions. In accordance with the Principle of Least Privilege, we recommend assigning only the specific permissions the bot needs to function. Our [default invite link](/invite) is pre-configured to request only the essential permissions required for Wamellow's features to operate correctly.

Wamellow interacts with your server via [Discord's official API](https://docs.discord.com) and does not have access to your personal account credentials. **Wamellow cannot send messages on your behalf or access your private data.**

If your server is experiencing an elevated number of phishing messages, you can attempt to mitigate these threats by [configuring Wamellow's AutoMod features](/profile?to=moderation).
